India Sugar Store par sugar ke liye online request submit ki ja sakti hai. Service 5 KG se 500 KG tak bulk quantity requests ke liye designed hai.

Online Request Kaise Karein?

  1. Quantity select karein.
  2. Apna naam, phone number aur delivery address enter karein.
  3. Request submit karein.
  4. Seller request ko review karke accept ya reject karta hai.

Request submit karna final order confirmation nahi hai. Delivery address aur availability seller review ke time check ki ja sakti hai.
